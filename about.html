Our Story

Meet Michael & Briggs

Mint & Barrel was born from a shared love of great cocktails, beautiful events, and the belief that the bar experience should be just as memorable as everything else on your wedding day.

As a team, Michael and Briggs bring professionalism, warmth, and craft to every event they serve. From the first consultation to the final pour, their goal is simple — to make your celebration feel effortless and extraordinary.

Fully licensed and insured, and proudly serving Utah County and Salt Lake County, Mint & Barrel is built on a foundation of trust, quality, and a genuine love for what they do.
